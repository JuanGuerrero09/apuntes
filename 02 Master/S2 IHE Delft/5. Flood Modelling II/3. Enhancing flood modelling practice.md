# Flood Forecast Assessment

Definition: Assessment is to determine the goodness-of-fit of the flood forecast

## Types of flood forecast

- 1-dimensional (point location and lead time)
- 2- or 3- dimensional (flooded area, flood extent, inundation depth and lead time)
- deterministic (single forecast of the flood and attribute)
- probabilistic

Main types of flood forecast assessment:
- Event-based verification
- Continuous verification
