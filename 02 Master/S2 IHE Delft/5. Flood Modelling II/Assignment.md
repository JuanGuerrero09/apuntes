## Catchment

The main catchment involve was the Rambla del Poyo catchment, even though it was called the valencia DANA the main city didn't have mayor inundations due the canalization of the River Turia, the one that mainly passes through the city.

On the other side, the towns on the lower part of the Rambla del Poyo basin were the one mainly affected. I got that catchemnt but it could be delineated better in QGIS (i did it with https://mghydro.com/watersheds/), but is possible to see how the catchment has a wide are to catch precipitation and then a narrow part is the one diverting to the albufera in the downstream part of the basin.

## Settlements

Mainly affected towns (in some upstream also died people because the water was 3 m height, however, I think that we could narrow the scope to the worst part, downstream) were the one in the downstream part of the basin: Paiporta (epicenter of the disaster), Picanya, Alfafar, Sedaví, Benetússer, Massanassa, Catarroja and Albal
![[Pasted image 20260611111716.png]]

The upper part of the basin in Chiva and Cheste received between 400 - 600 mm of precipitation, but idk if we should added as settlement affected since it was the upstream part or only mention it

Exposed assets are a major commercial center called Bonaire at mid-basin, and all the cars (around 140.000) that were completely damaged by the DANA, all the residential areas and roads that were directly inundated on the inundations. (We can ad more tho)

And indirect damages are the stopping of the electricity production of the nuclear plan of Cofrentes, since the DANA damaged several transmission towers and that could happen a sudden stop of the reactor
